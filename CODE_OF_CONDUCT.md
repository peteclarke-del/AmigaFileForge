# Code of conduct

Amiga File Forge welcomes careful technical contributions from people with
different levels of experience and different relationships with Amiga
hardware. Discussion should make it easier to understand the evidence and
improve the software.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, specific and patient.
- Critique code, behaviour and evidence, not the person reporting it.
- State assumptions and distinguish tested facts from recollection.
- Make room for questions from users who do not know the underlying filing
  systems.
- Respect privacy, copyright and responsible disclosure requirements.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to keep an unsafe or unproven operation
  read-only.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, deliberate
disruption, publication of private information, and sustained personal attacks
are not acceptable. Neither is pressuring contributors to publish firmware,
commercial software or private media they do not have permission to share.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `pete.clarke@gmail.com`. Include links or
screenshots where useful, but do not circulate the report publicly. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, temporarily
suspend access or permanently ban a participant. Reports will be considered
confidentially and conflicts of interest will be stated where they affect the
review.

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, review discussions and
project spaces where someone is representing Amiga File Forge.
